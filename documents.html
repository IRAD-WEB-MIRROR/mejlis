Resolution of the Mejlis · 5 December 2023 · No. 2023/12

On the standing committees of the Mejlis

In force

Forms the five standing committees of the chamber elected in 2023 (Ways and Means; Legal and Constitutional Affairs; Security and the Line; Social Affairs and Education; Cities and Districts), sets their competence and elects their chairmen and members. Each deputy other than the Speaker sits on one or two committees. The list of members by committee is kept in the Library of Legislative Hall. See The chamber.

Act · 29 September 2009 · No. 09-19

State Symbols Act 1993, as amended 2009

In force, as amended

Establishes the flag, the State Emblem and the State Anthem, The Spirit of Irad. The amendment passed by the Mejlis on 29 September 2009 and in force from 1 November 2009 introduced the State Emblem, used on the acts of the Mejlis.

Act · 17 June 2008 · No. 08-14

Customs Code of the ISEZ 2008

In force; amendment before the Mejlis

Sets ad valorem duties of 0, 5 or 10 per cent, duty-free regimes, the export duty on antimony concentrate and a transit regime with bonded storage yards. An amending bill on electronic declarations and storage periods passed its first reading on 20 October 2026; see Bills.

Territorial statute · 11 May 1993 · No. 93-09

Standard Time Act 1993

In force

Fixes Irad Time at UTC+6 for all official purposes, timetables and the sittings of the Mejlis. Summer time was abolished by the territorial legislature in 1996.

Territorial statute · 18 December 1992 · No. 92-11

Elective Governor Acts, as amended

In force, as amended in 2013

Provide for the election of the Governor and the Lieutenant Governor on a joint ticket for four years, on the same day as the deputies of the Mejlis; candidates must be at least 30 years of age with ten years' residence. Since the election of 2011 the term begins on the fourth Monday of November. The amendment passed by the chamber in 2013 removed the limit on the Governor's terms.

Basic law · 14 August 1992 · Organic Act (1992)

Organic Act of the Territory

In force as basic law

Organized the Territory of Irad in August 1992 and provided for an elected legislature, the Governor and the Supreme Court. Since 2007 the Organic Act and the territorial statutes are part of the basic law of the Zone, administered by the territorial institutions until a unification referendum.
