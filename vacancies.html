Vacancies

The Secretariat of the Mejlis announces competitions for vacant posts of the civil service. Posts are filled by competition under the Standing Orders and the staff regulations of the Secretariat.

Open competitions

MJ-26/18

Driver

Place of work
Personnel and Administration Department, Legislative Hall, Talas
Grade
Technical staff; 2,300–2,500 som a month
Applications until
13 November 2026

Duties: driving the service vehicles of the Secretariat in Talas and to the districts; carrying documents between Legislative Hall, Government House and the ministries; keeping the vehicle in good order.

Requirements: driving licence of category B and C; at least five years' driving without accidents; knowledge of the roads of the Talas valley and the districts; winter driving on the Fergana Gateway is an advantage.

MJ-26/14

Clerk of the Ways and Means Committee

Place of work
Committee Services Department, Legislative Hall, Talas
Grade
Civil service, senior grade 2; 3,450–3,900 som a month
Applications until
20 November 2026

Duties: preparing the agendas and papers of the committee, keeping the minutes of meetings and hearings, drafting the reports of the committee to the chamber and recording amendments; in November and December, work on the Budget Bill 2027 with the Ministry of Economy.

Requirements: higher education in economics, finance or law; at least three years' experience in the civil service or in a bank; good command of Iradi and English or Russian; ability to work late on sitting days.

MJ-26/15

Stenographer

Place of work
Records, Stenography and Library Department, Legislative Hall, Talas
Grade
Civil service, junior grade 1; 2,900–3,200 som a month
Applications until
27 November 2026

Duties: taking down the proceedings of sittings and committee hearings, preparing the stenographic transcript within two working days, checking quotations with the speakers and the sound recording.

MJ-26/16

Interpreter, Iradi–Russian–English (2 posts)

Place of work
Interpretation and Translation Section, Legislative Hall, Talas
Grade
Civil service, senior grade 3; 3,400–3,800 som a month
Applications until
4 December 2026

Duties: simultaneous interpretation of sittings and committee hearings; written translation of bills, acts, resolutions and the summary record; terminology of the budget and customs legislation.

MJ-26/17

Legal adviser

Place of work
Legal Department, Legislative Hall, Talas
Grade
Civil service, leading grade 2; 3,800–4,300 som a month
Applications until
15 December 2026

Duties: legal examination of bills and amendments for conformity with the Organic Act and the territorial statutes; drafting of resolutions of the Mejlis; opinions for the Speaker and the committees on the Standing Orders; preparing the final texts of acts for signature and the Gazette.

Requirements: higher legal education; at least five years' experience in legal drafting, the courts or the civil service; knowledge of the territorial statute book; fluent Iradi and English, Russian is an advantage.

How to apply

To take part in a competition, submit to the Personnel and Administration Department (Legislative Hall, room 112, north entrance, Monday to Friday 9:00–17:00) or by e-mail to kadr@mejlis.iradgov.org, not later than the closing date: an application addressed to the Chief of Staff with the reference of the post; a curriculum vitae with photograph; copies of the passport or residence card, of the diplomas and of the work record book; a certificate of no criminal record; a medical certificate.

The competition commission of the Secretariat examines the documents within 10 working days of the closing date. Candidates who meet the requirements are invited by telephone to a written test and an interview; the others are not contacted. The result is announced within 30 days of the closing date.

Enquiries: Personnel and Administration Department, +996-39-2-23-10-60. Documents submitted are not returned.
