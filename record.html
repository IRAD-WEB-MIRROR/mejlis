Record of sittings

A summary record of each sitting is published after it has been approved by the Speaker. The full transcript can be consulted in the Library of Legislative Hall.

2026-10-27
Road Fund (Amendment) Bill: report of the Cities and Districts Committee. Agreed.
Questions to the Minister of Economy, Dinara Usupova. Asked whether public pay and pensions would be indexed, the Minister said that wages are set in som and paid in som, and that the budget for 2027 would be laid in November.
2026-10-20
Customs Code (Amendment) Bill: first reading. Agreed, 54 votes to 3. Referred to the Ways and Means Committee.
Motion of thanks to the harvest workers of the Chu oasis. Agreed without division.
2026-10-06
Opening of the autumn session. The State Anthem. Address of the Lieutenant Governor, Aidar Maksat, on behalf of the Governor.
2026-04-14
Standards and Measures Bill: first reading. Agreed. Referred to the Ways and Means Committee.
Motion of three deputies for the Nurzhan city constituency for a hearing on the capacity of the Governor to discharge his office. Ruled out of order by the Speaker: "The chamber does not examine the health of citizens." No question put.
Points of order raised after the ruling are not entered in the record (Standing Order 12).

Records of sittings before 2026 are held at the State Archives.
