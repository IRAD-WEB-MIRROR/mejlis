Resolution of the Mejlis · 12 March 2024 · No. 2024/2

On the structure of the Secretariat (Apparatus) of the Mejlis

In force

1. The Secretariat (Apparatus) of the Mejlis provides for the work of the chamber, its committees and deputies and for the reception of citizens. It is headed by the Chief of Staff, appointed by the Speaker.

2. The Secretariat consists of: the Committee Services Department; the Legal Department; the Records, Stenography and Library Department; the Interpretation and Translation Section; the Press Service; the Department of Correspondence and Citizens' Appeals; the Personnel and Administration Department.

3. The staff establishment of the Secretariat is fixed at 118 posts, of which 84 are posts of the civil service.

4. Vacant posts of the civil service in the Secretariat are filled by competition.

5. The expenses of the Secretariat are met within the budget of the Mejlis.

6. Resolution No. 2012/4 on the Secretariat of the Mejlis is repealed. This resolution comes into force on 1 April 2024.
