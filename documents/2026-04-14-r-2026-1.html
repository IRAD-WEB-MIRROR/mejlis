Ruling of the Speaker (extract from the record) · 14 April 2026 · R-2026/1

Ruling on the motion for a hearing on the capacity of the Governor

Final (Standing Order 12)

Sitting of Tuesday 14 April 2026. Item of the order paper: motions.

1. Three deputies for the Nurzhan city constituency moved that the chamber hold a hearing on the capacity of the Governor to discharge his office.

2. The Speaker ruled the motion out of order: "The chamber does not examine the health of citizens."

3. No question was put.

4. Points of order raised after the ruling are not entered in the record (Standing Order 12).

Extract certified by the Records, Stenography and Library Department of the Secretariat.
