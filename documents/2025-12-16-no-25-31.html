Act · 16 December 2025 · No. 25-31

Budget Act 2026

In force, 1 January – 31 December 2026

1. The fiscal year 2026 is the calendar year from 1 January to 31 December 2026.

2. The revenue of the budget is approved at 32.4 billion som: royalties and profit tax of NurGaz about 36 per cent; sales tax about 15 per cent; customs, transit and the antimony export duty about 14 per cent; the share of the Zone in the export income of Toktogul about 12 per cent; income tax and other taxes about 10 per cent; fees from the United States installations and foreign firms about 8 per cent; other revenue about 5 per cent.

3. The spending of the budget is approved at 34.9 billion som: public wages about 34 per cent; security (the Gendarmerie, the Defense Corps, internal security) about 22 per cent; education about 12 per cent; roads and investment, including the royalty road fund of 1995, about 10 per cent; transfers to the Pension Fund and allowances about 9 per cent; health about 8 per cent; the reserve fund of the Governor about 5 per cent.

4. The deficit of the budget, 2.5 billion som, is covered from the Toktogul export escrow reserve held at Talasbank.

5. The budget is executed by the Ministry of Economy, which reports on its execution to the Ways and Means Committee each quarter.

6. This Act comes into force on 1 January 2026.
