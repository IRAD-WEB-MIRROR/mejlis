Resolution of the Mejlis · 23 June 2026 · No. 2026/19

On the calendar of the autumn session 2026

In force

1. The autumn session of the Mejlis shall open on Tuesday 6 October 2026 and close on Friday 18 December 2026.

2. Sittings shall be held on Tuesdays from 10:00 to 14:00. The closing sitting of the session shall be held on 18 December 2026 at 10:00.

3. The Budget Bill 2027 shall be received at the first sitting in November. The Ways and Means Committee shall hold its hearings on the bill in November and report to the chamber before the second reading. The third reading shall be held not later than the closing sitting of the session.

4. The standing committees shall meet on Wednesdays and Thursdays of the session weeks, and in the recess at the call of their chairmen.

5. The Secretariat shall publish this resolution and the order paper of each sitting on the website of the Mejlis.

6. This resolution comes into force on its adoption.
