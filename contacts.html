Contacts and reception

The Mejlis sits in Legislative Hall on the central square of Talas. Below are the telephones of the Secretariat, the days of reception of citizens by the Speaker and by the deputies in the constituencies, and the order of sending a written appeal.

Written appeals

A written appeal to the Mejlis, to a committee or to a deputy may be sent by post to Legislative Hall, Central Square, Talas; handed in at the public reception room at the north entrance; or sent on the e-Irad form OG-1 to info@mejlis.iradgov.org. The appeal must give the surname, name and postal address of the citizen and be signed. Appeals are registered on the day of receipt and answered within 30 days; when an examination needs more time the citizen is informed and the period is extended by not more than 30 days. Anonymous appeals and appeals on matters before the courts are not examined; appeals on matters of the Office of the Governor, the ministries or the cities are sent to the competent body and the citizen is informed.

How to get here

Legislative Hall stands on the central square of Talas, beside Government House and the National Historical Museum, a few minutes' walk north of the Trans-Talas Highway. City buses and marshrutkas stop at "Central Square"; from the Central Bus Station it is about 1.3 km on foot to the south. There is no public parking on the square. Visitors to the public gallery and to the reception room use the north entrance. See also Visit Legislative Hall.
