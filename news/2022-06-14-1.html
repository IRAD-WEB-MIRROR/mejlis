14 June 2022 · No. 22

Ramp and lift opened at the north entrance of Legislative Hall

In accordance with the executive directive of 2021 on ramps in public buildings, a ramp at the north entrance and a lift to the public gallery and the reception room of Legislative Hall have been put into service.

Two places in the public gallery are reserved for visitors in wheelchairs. Visitors who need assistance may inform the Secretariat in advance by telephone +996-39-2-23-10-27.

Press Service of the Mejlis
