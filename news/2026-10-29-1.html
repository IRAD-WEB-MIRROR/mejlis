Budget Bill 2027 to be laid on 3 November

The Office of the Governor will lay the Budget Bill 2027 before the Mejlis at the sitting of Tuesday 3 November 2026. After the first reading the bill goes to the Ways and Means Committee, which will hold hearings with the Ministry of Economy and the other ministries in November. The final reading is expected in December, before the close of the session on 18 December.

Journalists who wish to attend the hearings of the committee are asked to apply for accreditation to the Press Service at press@mejlis.iradgov.org.

Press Service of the Mejlis
