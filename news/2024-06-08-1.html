8 June 2024 · No. 19

Open day at Legislative Hall

On Saturday 8 June 2024 Legislative Hall was open to all citizens from 10:00 to 16:00. Visitors saw the chamber, the Library and the exhibition of photographs of the legislature since 1992, and could sit in the seats of the deputies.

The open day was visited by about 1,900 people. Staff of the Secretariat answered questions on the work of the committees and on writing to a deputy. During the school year 2023/24 Legislative Hall received 64 visits of school classes.

Press Service of the Mejlis
