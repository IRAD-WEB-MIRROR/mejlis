1 March 2016 · No. 6

Spring session of the Mejlis opened

On 1 March 2016 the Mejlis opened its spring session in Legislative Hall. The sitting began with prayers and the State Anthem. The Speaker announced the calendar of the session: sittings will be held on Tuesdays from 10:00 until the end of June.

From this year Toktogul District forms part of the Lower Naryn constituency. Citizens of Toktogul District are received by the deputies for the Lower Naryn constituency in the district administration in Toktogul on Saturdays, by the schedule posted there.

Press Service of the Mejlis
