Spring session closed: results

On 23 June 2026 the Mejlis held the last sitting of the spring session. In the session the Mejlis held 17 sittings, passed 9 Acts at the third reading, agreed to 3 bills at the first reading and adopted 21 resolutions. Ministers answered questions of deputies at 11 sittings.

At the same sitting the Mejlis adopted the resolution on the calendar of the autumn session: the session will open on 6 October and close on 18 December 2026. The committees continue their work in the recess.

Press Service of the Mejlis
