24 March 2020 · No. 9

Public gallery closed; sittings continue

In connection with the measures against the spread of infection, from 24 March 2020 the public gallery of Legislative Hall is closed and the visits of schools and groups are suspended until further notice.

Sittings of the Mejlis continue on Tuesdays. The deputies are seated with free places between them. Personal reception of citizens is replaced by reception by telephone; written appeals are accepted by post and at info@mejlis.iradgov.org.

Press Service of the Mejlis
