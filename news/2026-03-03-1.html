3 March 2026 · No. 4

Spring session 2026 opened

On 3 March 2026 the Mejlis opened its spring session. After prayers and the State Anthem the Lieutenant Governor, Aidar Maksat, addressed the chamber on behalf of the Governor.

From this session the summary record of each sitting is published on this website after it has been approved by the Speaker. Records of earlier sittings are held at the State Archives; the full transcript can be consulted in the Library of Legislative Hall.

Press Service of the Mejlis
