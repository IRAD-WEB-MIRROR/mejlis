25 September 2018 · No. 33

Repairs of the roof and the public gallery completed

During the summer recess the roof of Legislative Hall over the chamber was renewed and the ceiling of the chamber restored. The seats of the public gallery were repaired and the north entrance repainted.

The works were carried out within the budget of the Mejlis for 2018. The public gallery will be open from the first sitting of the autumn session.

Press Service of the Mejlis
