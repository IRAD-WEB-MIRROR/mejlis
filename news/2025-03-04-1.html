4 March 2025 · No. 5

Spring session opened. Sittings broadcast live on Radio Irad

On 4 March 2025 the Mejlis opened its spring session with the State Anthem. The session will last until the end of June; sittings are held on Tuesdays from 10:00 to 14:00.

From this session every sitting is broadcast live on the Radio Irad network, with the original speech and without interpretation. A new sound system with microphones at each seat was installed in the chamber during the winter recess.

Press Service of the Mejlis
