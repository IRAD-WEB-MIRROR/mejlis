19 December 2017 · No. 48

Budget for 2018 passed at the third reading

At the sitting of 19 December 2017 the Mejlis passed the Budget Bill 2018 at the third reading. The report of the Ways and Means Committee was presented by its chairman, Nurbek Sultanov, deputy for the Talas city constituency.

The bill was laid by the Office of the Governor in November and examined by the committee at hearings with the Ministry of Economy. The Act is sent to the Governor for signature and will be published in the Gazette.

Press Service of the Mejlis
