17 September 2026 · No. 29

Ways and Means Committee discusses electronic customs declarations

On 17 September 2026 the Ways and Means Committee held a working meeting with the Customs Service and the Ministry of Economy on the draft Customs Code (Amendment) Bill, which provides for electronic declarations at all main crossings and longer storage in bonded yards.

The chairman of the committee informed the members of the meeting held earlier the same week with the head of the Border and Customs Representation of the Republic of Kazakhstan in Nurzhan on the exchange of declaration data at Chu Gate. The bill is to be laid at the beginning of the autumn session.
