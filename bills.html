Bills before the Mejlis

Autumn session, 6 October to 18 December 2026. A bill is read three times; between the first and second readings it goes to a committee. Acts are signed by the Governor and published in the Gazette.

Budget Bill 2027

Office of the Governor

To be laid on 3 November 2026. Ways and Means Committee hearings in November; final reading in December.

Road Fund (Amendment) Bill

Office of the Governor

Allows the royalty road fund of 1995 to pay for the upkeep of district roads. Second reading 3 November 2026.

Standards and Measures Bill

Ministry of Economy

Puts Iradstandart, the State Agency for Standards, Metrology and Certification, on a statutory footing. In committee.

Customs Code (Amendment) Bill

Ministry of Economy

Electronic declarations at all main crossings; longer storage in bonded yards. First reading passed 20 October 2026.

The budget cycle

The fiscal year is the calendar year. The Ministry of Economy drafts the budget, the Office of the Governor lays it before the Mejlis in November and it is passed in December. The 2026 budget provided for revenue of 32.4 billion som and spending of 34.9 billion som.
